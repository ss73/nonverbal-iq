### Promotional text [170]

Sixteen fresh puzzles, ten minutes, no words needed. Find the pattern, beat the clock, and see exactly why each answer is right.

### Description [4000]

How well can you spot a pattern? Nonverbal IQ is a 10-minute reasoning test built from shapes, not words — so no vocabulary, no trivia and no language barrier.

THREE CLASSIC PUZZLE TYPES
• Progressive matrices — find the figure that completes a 3×3 grid, the classic format used in admission tests by high-IQ societies.
• Figure series — work out how a sequence evolves and pick what comes next.
• Odd one out — spot the figure that breaks the rule the others follow.

A FAIR, FOCUSED TEST
• 16 puzzles per round, from easy to genuinely hard.
• A 10-minute clock for the round, but no limit per puzzle — skip, go back and change answers freely.
• Submit whenever you're ready.
• Every round is generated fresh, so you never see the same test twice.

LEARN FROM EVERY ANSWER
• Get an estimated IQ score with a likely range and where it places you in the population.
• Review every puzzle: see your answer, the correct one, and a step-by-step explanation of the reasoning.
• Track your results over time on the home screen.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Your results stay on your device — nothing is ever sent anywhere.
• Works completely offline.

The score is an estimate from a short test, meant for fun and self-reflection. It is not a clinical or officially administered IQ assessment.
